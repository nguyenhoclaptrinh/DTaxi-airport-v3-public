# Kịch bản mô phỏng DTaxi (Standard ICAO Phraseology)

## 1. Kịch bản Cất cánh (Departure - PC-01)

1. **Ready for Startup/Pushback**: `PC01: READY FOR STARTUP AND PUSHBACK, STAND A1`
2. **Startup/Pushback Approved**: `ATC: PUSHBACK APPROVED, FACE SOUTH, QNH 1013`
3. **Request Taxi**: `PC01: READY TO TAXI`
4. **Taxi Instruction**: `ATC: TAXI TO HOLDING POINT RWY 25L VIA A, B`
5. **Holding Short**: `PC01: HOLDING SHORT RWY 25L`
6. **Line up and Wait**: `ATC: LINE UP AND WAIT RWY 25L`
7. **Takeoff Clearance**: `ATC: RWY 25L, CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF`
8. **Airborne Report**: `PC01: AIRBORNE, PASSING 1500 FEET`
9. **Contact Departure**: `ATC: CONTACT DEPARTURE 120.1, GOOD DAY`

## 2. Kịch bản Hạ cánh (Arrival - PC-02)

1. **Initial Contact/Approach**: `PC02: REQUEST LANDING DATA, 10 MILES OUT`
2. **Landing Clearance**: `ATC: RWY 25L, CLEARED TO LAND, WIND 240/10`
3. **Touchdown Report**: `PC02: TOUCHDOWN RWY 25L`
4. **Vacate Instruction**: `ATC: VACATE RWY VIA TAXIWAY D, CONTACT GROUND`
5. **Runway Vacated**: `PC02: RUNWAY VACATED AT D`
6. **Taxi in Instruction**: `ATC: TAXI TO STAND B5 VIA D, E`
7. **Approaching Stand**: `PC02: APPROACHING STAND B5`
8. **Arrived Stand (On Block)**: `PC02: ON BLOCK, STAND B5`
9. **Engines Shutdown**: `ATC: SHUTDOWN APPROVED, GOOD DAY`
